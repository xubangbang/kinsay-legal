Privacy Policy

Effective: October 25, 2026 · Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Joe, the developer and operator of Kinsay ("we," "us," or "our"), handles personal information when you use the Kinsay app and these legal pages. Contact us at heyjoewynn@gmail.com.

The essentials. We use your account details and the content you share to run Kinsay. Conversations are available to members of the Space where you share them. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Deleting your account removes your profile and erases your original shared text, including contributions in Spaces you have left.

1. Information we handle

  • Account and profile information: your email address, chosen username, account identifier, sign-in method, and account creation or update dates.
  • Information from Apple or Google: if you choose either sign-in method, we receive the information needed to authenticate you, such as a provider identifier, email address, and authentication tokens. Apple may provide a private relay email address. Google may also supply basic profile information, such as a name or profile picture reference, as part of its identity information. We do not receive your Apple or Google password or request access to your contacts, calendar, or email inbox.
  • Content and group information: the text you share and reply with, Space names, memberships, invitation codes and their status, author identifiers, timestamps, and conversation relationships. Information you choose to include in a message is part of that content.
  • Technical and security information: our service providers may process IP addresses, request and sign-in timestamps, device or client information, and error or security logs when your device connects. We record invitation attempts to limit misuse.
  • Information on your device: sign-in credentials used to keep you signed in, the sign-in method, and preferences such as dismissed invitation reminders. The app does not maintain a persistent local archive of conversations.
  • Safety information: member blocks, report reasons and content or account identifiers, review outcomes, and account restrictions. Reports do not create a separate copy of the original message text. Authorized operators may review reported content and relevant context to respond to abuse.
  • Support correspondence: your email address and information you send when you contact us.

The current app does not request access to your photo library, camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location. It does not display advertising. When you choose Google Sign-In, Google may process contact information, account and device identifiers, usage and other technical data for authentication, security, and analytics. Google may estimate a general location from IP addresses to prevent fraud. Google’s privacy terms apply to its sign-in service. We do not use conversations for advertising or add a separate product analytics service. These legal pages do not add analytics, advertising scripts, or cookies; GitHub, their host, may process technical information about visits under its own privacy statement.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and authenticate your account; display your profile; let you create, join, and use Spaces; deliver shared text and replies; manage invitations; keep the service secure; troubleshoot problems; answer requests; and meet legal obligations. We do not use your conversations for advertising.

Where a legal basis is required, we process information as necessary to provide the service you request, for legitimate interests in operating and protecting the service and answering support requests, or to comply with legal obligations. Where consent is required, we rely on consent for that specific processing. You may withdraw that consent by contacting us, without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

3. Who can access information

Other members. Members of the same Space can see its shared content, member usernames, and relevant conversation details. People who join later can see existing conversations. Invitation codes can be reused while valid, so share them only with people you intend to invite. Your account email is not displayed in the member list. Other members can copy or capture anything they can view, and we cannot control their copies.

Service providers. We use Supabase for account authentication and hosting app data, together with the email delivery services used for sign-in codes. Apple and Google process information when you choose their sign-in services. GitHub Pages hosts these legal pages. Providers process the information needed for their functions; their own services may also be subject to their privacy terms.

If operation of Kinsay is transferred, information may transfer to a successor subject to applicable law and appropriate notice.

4. Retention and deletion

We keep account information while your account is active and shared content while it remains part of a Space, subject to the controls below. Open reports are kept while being investigated; closed report metadata is removed through regular queue maintenance after 30 days. Deleting the relevant account removes its reports, block records and account restrictions from the active service. Other support and security records are kept only as long as needed to resolve the relevant issue, protect the service, or meet legal obligations. Backup and infrastructure records may remain until replaced or removed under the applicable provider's retention schedule; deletion from the active service does not necessarily erase those records immediately.

  • Delete a message: while you are a member, you can delete your own shared text or replies through the app. Deleting a top-level message also removes its replies. Deleting a reply removes its text; an empty placeholder may remain to preserve other people's replies.
  • Leave a Space: leaving removes your membership and access. It does not delete your previous messages or replies.
  • Delete a Space you created: this removes that Space, its memberships and invitations, and everyone's messages and replies in it.
  • Delete your account: open Settings → Delete Account and confirm. This removes your authentication account, profile, and memberships from the active service. It also deletes every Space you created and all conversations in those Spaces. Text you contributed to Spaces created by others is permanently erased, including contributions in Spaces you have left. Empty deleted placeholders may remain to keep other people’s replies connected.

Deleting your account erases your original message text from the active service; it does not delete other people’s replies in their Spaces. You may also contact us for assistance with your applicable privacy rights. Copies saved by other members are outside our control. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

Deleting your Kinsay account does not delete your Apple or Google account. For Apple-linked accounts, Kinsay asks you to confirm with Apple and revokes Kinsay’s Apple authorization before completing deletion. You can also manage access in your provider’s account settings.

5. Your choices and rights

You can change your username in Settings, edit or delete your own content while you retain access, sign out, leave Spaces you joined, and delete your account. The current app does not support changing your account email.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, receive a copy of, correct, delete, or restrict use of personal information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data protection authority. To make a request, email heyjoewynn@gmail.com, preferably from the address linked to your account. We may need to verify your identity and will respond within applicable legal deadlines. Some requests are subject to legal exceptions and the rights of other people.

6. Security and international processing

We use authenticated connections and membership-based access controls to protect app data. No internet service or storage system can guarantee complete security. Keep your device, sign-in account, email codes, and invitation codes secure.

Our providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live, where privacy laws may differ. Where applicable law requires international transfer safeguards, those requirements apply to the processing described here. Contact us for information about the providers and safeguards relevant to your request.

7. Children

Kinsay is not directed to children under 13, or a higher minimum age where required by local law. We do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child below the applicable minimum age has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Kinsay changes. We will post the revised version here with a new “Last updated” date and provide additional notice when required by law.

For privacy questions or requests, contact Joe, Kinsay's operator, at heyjoewynn@gmail.com.
