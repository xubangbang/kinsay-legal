Privacy Policy

Effective: October 25, 2026 · Last updated: November 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Joe, the developer and operator of Kinsay ("we," "us," or "our"), handles personal information when you use the Kinsay app and these legal pages. Contact us at support@kinsayapp.com.

The essentials. We use your account details, subscription status, and the text, photos, and voice messages you share to run Kinsay. Conversations and media are available to members of the Space where you share them. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Deleting your account removes your profile and permanently erases your original shared text, photos, and voice messages, including contributions in Spaces you have left.

1. Information we handle

  • Account and profile information: your email address, chosen username, account identifier, sign-in method, and account creation or update dates.
  • Information from Apple or Google: if you choose either sign-in method, we receive the information needed to authenticate you, such as a provider identifier, email address, and authentication tokens. Apple may provide a private relay email address. Google may also supply basic profile information, such as a name or profile picture reference, as part of its identity information. We do not receive your Apple or Google password or request access to your contacts, calendar, or email inbox.
  • Content and group information: the text you share and reply with, Space names, memberships, invitation codes and their status, author identifiers, timestamps, and conversation relationships. In Spaces covered by Kinsay Plus, this also includes the photos (up to 9 per post or reply, resized and compressed on your device with embedded photo metadata stripped before upload) and voice messages (AAC audio recordings up to 3 minutes, along with duration and file metadata) that you choose to share. Information you choose to include in a message, photo, or voice recording is part of that content.
  • Subscription and purchase information: if you subscribe to Kinsay Plus or restore purchases through Apple In-App Purchase, we store Apple-verified subscription metadata linked to your account—including Apple's original transaction identifier, product identifier, subscription status, free-trial and auto-renewal flags, and expiration, grace-period, or revocation timestamps—to activate Kinsay Plus benefits for you and up to 2 of your Spaces. Apple processes payment directly; we do not receive your payment card details, billing address, or Apple Account password.
  • Unread activity and notifications: we store which discussions you have read and your notification preferences. If you enable notifications, we store an app installation identifier, an Apple push token, and the information needed to associate that installation with your signed-in account and deliver alerts and badges. For eligible 7-day free trials, the app may also schedule a local on-device reminder notification two days before the trial ends.
  • Technical and security information: our service providers may process IP addresses, request and sign-in timestamps, device or client information, and error or security logs when your device connects. We record invitation attempts to limit misuse.
  • Information on your device: sign-in credentials used to keep you signed in, the sign-in method, preferences such as dismissed invitation reminders, temporary draft voice recordings stored in your device's temporary directory until sent, canceled, or automatically cleared, and short-lived in-memory caches (or temporary playback files cleared when playback stops) for media you view or play. The app does not maintain a persistent local archive of conversations.
  • Safety information: member blocks, report reasons and content or account identifiers, review outcomes, and account restrictions. Reports do not create a separate copy of the original message text or media. Authorized operators may review reported content (including reported photos or voice messages) and relevant context to respond to abuse.
  • Support correspondence: your email address and information you send when you contact us.

Device permissions and media access. Kinsay requests access to your microphone only when you choose to record a voice message; audio is captured only while you actively record and is uploaded only if you send the message. To attach photos, Kinsay uses Apple's system photo picker (PhotosPicker), which lets you select individual images without granting Kinsay broad access to your photo library. The app does not request access to your camera, full photo library, contacts, or precise location. It does not display advertising. When you choose Google Sign-In, Google may process contact information, account and device identifiers, usage and other technical data for authentication, security, and analytics. Google may estimate a general location from IP addresses to prevent fraud. Google’s privacy terms apply to its sign-in service. We do not use conversations, photos, or voice messages for advertising or add a separate product analytics service. These legal pages do not add analytics, advertising scripts, or cookies; Cloudflare Pages (and GitHub Pages, which continues to host legacy legal pages for earlier app versions) may process technical information about visits under their own privacy policies.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and authenticate your account; display your profile; let you create, join, and use Spaces; deliver shared text, photos, voice messages, and replies; verify Apple In-App Purchase subscriptions and provide shared Kinsay Plus Space entitlements; manage invitations; keep the service secure; troubleshoot problems; answer requests; and meet legal obligations. We do not use your conversations, photos, or voice messages for advertising or AI model training.

We use unread activity to keep your indicators in sync across devices. If you enable notifications, we use your device registration to deliver alerts about activity in your Spaces, and may schedule an optional local reminder before a 7-day free trial renews.

Where a legal basis is required, we process information as necessary to provide the service you request, for legitimate interests in operating and protecting the service and answering support requests, or to comply with legal obligations. Where consent is required, we rely on consent for that specific processing. You may withdraw that consent by contacting us, without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

3. Who can access information

Other members. Members of the same Space can see its shared text, photos, voice messages, member usernames, and relevant conversation details, including historical photos and voice messages if a Space's Kinsay Plus coverage later ends. People who join later can see existing conversations and media. Invitation codes can be reused while valid, so share them only with people you intend to invite. Your account email, Apple Account, and personal billing details are not displayed to other Space members. Other members can copy, save, or capture anything they can view or hear, and we cannot control their copies.

Service providers. We use Supabase for account authentication, database hosting, and private object storage for shared photos and voice messages (downloaded by authorized Space members through authenticated, membership- and block-checked requests over encrypted connections), together with the email delivery services used for sign-in codes. Apple processes Sign in with Apple, push notifications, and In-App Purchase subscriptions (including App Store Server Notifications V2 for renewal, grace period, expiration, and refund updates). Google processes information when you choose Sign in with Google. Cloudflare Pages hosts the Kinsay website and legal pages, and GitHub Pages continues to host legacy legal pages for earlier app versions. Providers process the information needed for their functions; their own services may also be subject to their privacy terms.

Push notifications. Apple receives your push token and the information needed to deliver a generic alert, update the badge, and open the relevant discussion. Push alerts do not include your message text, photos, audio, username, or Space name.

4. Retention and deletion

We keep account information while your account is active and shared content (including photos and voice messages) while it remains part of a Space, subject to the controls below. Shared photos and voice messages are stored in private, non-public object storage accessible only through authenticated requests after server-side membership and block checks. Abandoned media uploads that are never attached to a sent message are automatically queued for physical removal. Open reports are kept while being investigated; closed report metadata is removed through regular queue maintenance after 30 days. Deleting the relevant account removes its reports, block records and account restrictions from the active service. Other support and security records are kept only as long as needed to resolve the relevant issue, protect the service, or meet legal obligations. Backup and infrastructure records may remain until replaced or removed under the applicable provider's retention schedule; deletion from the active service does not necessarily erase those records immediately.

Leaving a Space removes the read state for that membership. The notification service regularly removes expired delivery metadata and inactive device registrations. Signing out detaches the device from your account when the app can reach the service. You can turn off alerts in iOS Settings and change your alert preferences in Kinsay.

  • Delete a message: while you are a member, you can delete your own shared posts or replies through the app. Deleting a top-level post also removes its replies and queues all associated photos and voice messages for physical deletion from storage. Deleting a reply removes its text and media (queuing the files for physical deletion); an empty placeholder may remain to preserve other people's replies.
  • Leave a Space: leaving removes your membership and access. It does not delete your previous messages, photos, voice messages, or replies.
  • Delete a Space you created: this removes that Space, its memberships and invitations, and everyone's messages, replies, photos, and voice messages in it, queuing all stored media files in that Space for physical deletion.
  • Delete your account: open Settings → Delete Account and confirm. Before removing your account, the service physically deletes all stored photo and voice files in every Space you created (from all members) as well as every photo and voice file you uploaded in any other Space, including Spaces you have left. Account deletion then removes your authentication account, profile, memberships, and every Space you created from the active service, and permanently erases your original text in other Spaces. Empty deleted placeholders may remain to keep other people’s replies connected.

Deleting your account erases your original message text, photos, and voice messages from the active service; it does not delete other people’s replies or media in their Spaces. Subscription transaction records are detached from your deleted account (clearing your user identifier) so that if you still have an active Apple subscription and later create a new Kinsay account, you can re-link it via Restore Purchases. You may also contact us for assistance with your applicable privacy rights. Copies saved by other members are outside our control. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account or cancel any Apple subscription.

Deleting your Kinsay account does not delete your Apple or Google account and does not automatically cancel an active Apple subscription; manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple Subscription Settings. For Apple-linked accounts, Kinsay asks you to confirm with Apple and revokes Kinsay’s Apple authorization before completing deletion. You can also manage access in your provider’s account settings.

5. Your choices and rights

You can change your username in Settings, edit or delete your own content while you retain access, manage or cancel a Kinsay Plus subscription in Apple Subscription Settings, revoke microphone or notification permissions in iOS Settings, sign out, leave Spaces you joined, and delete your account. The current app does not support changing your account email.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, receive a copy of, correct, delete, or restrict use of personal information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data protection authority. To make a request, email support@kinsayapp.com, preferably from the address linked to your account. We may need to verify your identity and will respond within applicable legal deadlines. Some requests are subject to legal exceptions and the rights of other people.

6. Security and international processing

We use authenticated connections and membership-based access controls to protect app data. No internet service or storage system can guarantee complete security. Keep your device, sign-in account, email codes, and invitation codes secure.

Our providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live, where privacy laws may differ. Where applicable law requires international transfer safeguards, those requirements apply to the processing described here. Contact us for information about the providers and safeguards relevant to your request.

7. Children

Kinsay is not directed to children under 13, or a higher minimum age where required by local law. We do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child below the applicable minimum age has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Kinsay changes. We will post the revised version here with a new “Last updated” date and provide additional notice when required by law.

For privacy questions or requests, contact Joe, Kinsay's operator, at support@kinsayapp.com.
